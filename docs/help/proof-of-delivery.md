# Proof of delivery

A shipment can only be marked Delivered after the assigned courier captures proof of delivery at the door.

## What the courier captures

The courier records the recipient's name and adds a photo of the handover, the recipient's signature, or both. If drawing a signature is not possible, the recipient's name can be typed instead. Proof can only be added while the shipment is Out for delivery, and it is locked once the shipment is delivered.

## Privacy of delivery photos

Photos are shrunk on the courier's phone before upload, which also removes location data from the image. They are stored privately. Only the shipment's customer, the assigned courier and admins can view the proof.

## Seeing the proof

After delivery, customers and admins see the recipient's name, the photo and the signature on the shipment page.
