# AI features

ExpressHub has optional AI features. They only appear when the ExpressHub team has switched them on.

## Shipment assistant

Signed-in customers and couriers can ask the assistant about their own shipments and payments in plain English or Bangla, for example where a parcel is or whether a payment went through. The assistant only sees what your account is allowed to see and cannot change anything.

## Booking from a description

When booking, customers can describe the parcel in their own words and the form fills itself in for review. Nothing is booked until you check the details and confirm.

## Help chat

The help chat on public pages answers general questions using only these help articles, and shows which articles it used. It cannot see your shipments or account. If the articles do not cover a question, it says so instead of guessing.
