# Getting started with ExpressHub

ExpressHub is an express courier service in Bangladesh. You book a parcel online, pay by card, and follow it from pickup to the recipient's door.

## Creating an account

Register with your name, email address and a password of at least 12 characters. New accounts are customer accounts. Customers can book shipments, pay for them, follow them and cancel them before pickup.

## Who does what

There are three kinds of account:

- Customers book and pay for shipments and see only their own shipments and payments.
- Couriers see the shipments assigned to them and update their progress on the road.
- Admins run operations: they assign couriers, manage users, handle failed deliveries and returns, and see reports.

Courier and admin accounts are set up by the ExpressHub team. You cannot change your own role.

## Where to go next

Book your first parcel from Shipments, then New shipment. After booking you pay with Stripe, and the shipment page shows the price, the status timeline and a tracking link you can share.
