# Booking a shipment

Customers book a shipment from Shipments, then New shipment. Booking has four steps: Pickup, Delivery, Parcel and Review.

## Pickup and delivery details

For both the pickup and the delivery you enter a contact name, a phone number and the full address. Names need at least 2 characters and addresses at least 5. Each step checks its own fields, and going back keeps what you typed.

## Parcel details

Enter the parcel weight in kilograms; it can have up to three decimal places. A package description and the length, width and height in centimetres are optional. You can also choose a pickup time, which must be in the future and is entered in your device's time zone.

## Describe your parcel in your own words

If the AI features are switched on, you can describe the parcel in plain English or Bangla, for example who it goes to and how heavy it is. The form fills itself in for you to check. Nothing is booked until you review the details and confirm.

## After you book

The shipment is created with the status Created and a tracking number such as EH- followed by 24 letters and numbers. The final price is shown on the shipment page. A courier can only pick the parcel up after the payment is confirmed.
