# Email notifications

ExpressHub emails customers when something important happens to their shipment.

## Which emails you get

You get an email when your shipment is booked, picked up, out for delivery, delivered, or when a delivery fails. Each email includes a link to the live tracking page. Courier assignment and cancellations do not send an email.

## Turning emails off

Open Profile and switch off shipment status emails. Emails that are still queued are skipped once the setting is off.

## If an email does not arrive

Emails are queued at the same moment as the status change and sent right after, so one is never lost or sent twice. Failed sends are retried several times, and admins can see and retry emails that failed every attempt. Check your spam folder and that your profile email address is correct.
