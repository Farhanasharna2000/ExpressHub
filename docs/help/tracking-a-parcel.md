# Tracking a parcel

Anyone with a tracking number can follow a shipment on the public tracking page. No account is needed.

## How to track

Enter the tracking number in the Track a parcel box on the home page, or open the tracking link directly. A tracking number looks like EH- followed by 24 letters and numbers. Customers can copy the tracking link from the shipment page with Copy tracking link and share it with the recipient.

## Live updates

The tracking page updates by itself when the status changes, without reloading. If the connection drops it shows Reconnecting and tries again.

## What the tracking page shows

The page shows the current status, when the shipment was booked and delivered, and the timeline of status changes with the hub city where known. To protect privacy it never shows names, phone numbers, addresses, prices or notes. Archived shipments and unknown tracking numbers show a page saying the shipment could not be found.
