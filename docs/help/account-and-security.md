# Your account and security

Manage your details from Profile.

## Profile details

You can change your name, phone number and email address, and upload or remove a profile photo.

## Changing your email address

Changing your email address requires your current password. After the change, you are signed out on your other devices and stay signed in on the one you used.

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must be at least 12 characters. Repeated failed sign-in attempts are limited, both per device and per account, to slow down password guessing. If an old session token is reused, ExpressHub signs that account out everywhere as a precaution.

## Signing out

Signing out ends the session on that device. Accounts that an admin deactivates can no longer sign in.
