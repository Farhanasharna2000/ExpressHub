# Guide for couriers

Couriers work from the Courier area, which can be installed on a phone like an app.

## Your shipments

You see only the shipments assigned to you. Admins assign couriers before pickup, or after a failed delivery for another attempt.

## Updating a shipment

Move each shipment forward as you go: Picked up, In transit, Out for delivery, then Delivered or Failed. Pickup and later steps need the payment to be confirmed. Delivered and Failed need a note. You cannot cancel shipments, retry failed deliveries or return parcels; an admin does that.

## Delivering with proof

At the door, capture the recipient's name and a photo, a signature, or both, then mark the shipment Delivered. If you are offline, the proof waits on your phone and uploads when the connection returns. The app shows Waiting to upload until it is sent.

## Installing the courier app

On the live site, open the Courier area on your phone and choose Add to Home Screen.
